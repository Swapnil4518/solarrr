How solar power works

1. Panels catch sunlight

Solar cells made of silicon absorb light and release electrons, creating direct current (DC).

2. An inverter converts it

The inverter changes DC into alternating current (AC), the kind your appliances use.

3. You use or store it

Power runs your home, charges a battery, or flows back to the grid.

Buy solar with a government subsidy

In India, homeowners can get a central subsidy on rooftop solar through the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana. You apply yourself on the official portal and the subsidy is paid into your bank account after your system is installed and inspected.

System sizeCentral subsidy
1 kW₹30,000
2 kW₹60,000
3 kW and above₹78,000 (maximum)

Who can apply?

  • You own a house with a roof suitable for solar panels
  • You have a valid, active electricity connection
  • You have not taken any other rooftop solar subsidy

Keep these ready

  • Recent electricity bill with your consumer number
  • Mobile number linked to your electricity account
  • Bank account details and a cancelled cheque
  1. Register on the portalChoose your state and electricity company, then enter your consumer number, mobile and email.
  2. Log in and applyUse your consumer number and mobile number to fill in the rooftop solar application.
  3. Get approval and installAfter your electricity company approves, a registered vendor installs the system.
  4. Net metering and inspectionYour meter is changed so extra power can go to the grid, and the plant is checked.
  5. Receive your subsidyThe money comes straight to your bank account.
Apply on official portal Scheme details Helpline 15555

Subsidy amounts and rules can change, and some states add their own top-up. Always confirm on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before you buy. This website is independent and is not a government site.

Before you buy: a quick checklist

  • Use registered vendors. Pick an installer listed on the official portal.
  • Compare three quotes. Check price per kW, what is included and the timeline.
  • Check the roof. You need open, shade-free space, ideally facing south.
  • Ask about warranty. Get it in writing for panels, inverter and workmanship.
  • Use Indian-made panels. The subsidy scheme requires modules and cells made in India.
  • Plan for care. Clean the panels now and then and watch your monthly output.

Common questions

Does solar work at night or on cloudy days?

Panels make less power in clouds and none at night. With a grid-connected system you simply draw from the grid when needed, and net metering credits the extra power you send out during the day.

Is the subsidy paid to me or to the vendor?

It is paid by direct bank transfer to the account you provide, after your system is installed and inspected.

Can shops, factories or farms get this subsidy?

This scheme is for homes and housing societies. Businesses and farms use other schemes, for example PM-KUSUM for farmers. See mnre.gov.in for details.

How much free electricity can I get?

The scheme aims to give eligible households up to 300 units of free electricity a month, depending on the size of the system and your usage.
